AI Safety

Helpful AI with firm medical boundaries.

Remi explains, organizes, and routes health information. It does not diagnose users or replace clinical care.

NoDiagnosis
YesUrgency tiers

Chat safety

Non-diagnostic companion prompts

Symptom conversations use plain language, limited follow-up questions, urgency tiers, and a clear recommendation to see a doctor when appropriate.

Documents

Classification before routing

Uploaded items are classified by type first, then routed to the correct workflow. Prescriptions always require confirmation before saving.

Images

Raw scans are not interpreted

Scan images can be stored for doctor review, but Remi does not visually interpret raw scan images.

Reliability

Multi-provider failover

AI providers are routed through a shared interface with circuit breakers, admin incidents, and narrower chains for safety-critical calls.

Designed to ask for help instead of guessing.

Low-confidence classifications ask the user to confirm. Safety-critical failures return deterministic safe responses instead of silently trusting unverified providers.

Remi keeps AI useful by keeping it bounded.

The product is built around routing, explanation, confirmation, and clinician follow-up.
