Built for health safety from the start.

Remi is designed as a companion, not a doctor. The system keeps human confirmation and urgent-care routing in the places that matter.

Non-diagnostic chat

Remi explains symptoms and prepares users for care conversations without claiming a likely diagnosis.

Prescription confirmation

Prescription scans always go through a user confirmation screen before medication records are saved.

Scan image protection

Raw scan images are stored for doctor review and are not visually interpreted by AI.

Provider failover

AI provider health is monitored with circuit breakers, incident logs, and admin alerts.

Common questions.

A quick view of how Remi positions itself for users, caregivers, and operators.

Is Remi a doctor?

No. Remi is a daily health companion. It can explain terms, organize information, and recommend seeing a doctor, but it does not diagnose conditions.

Can Remi save prescriptions from chat?

It can route a prescription image to the confirmation flow, but it does not auto-save medication details without the user confirming every field.

What happens to scan images?

Raw scan images are saved to scan history for doctor review. Remi does not interpret the scan image itself.

Can admins see private health text?

The admin dashboard is designed around operational signals and redacted data, not full symptom descriptions or exact private readings.
