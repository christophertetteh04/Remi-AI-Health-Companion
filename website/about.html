About Remi

A companion for the health details people forget between visits.

Remi is built around a simple idea: everyday health notes become more useful when they are easy to capture, privately stored, and ready for the next care conversation.

Mission

Make personal health tracking feel less overwhelming.

Remi is not a doctor. It is a daily support layer that helps users organize what they notice, remember what they planned, and prepare for clinical conversations.

Calm by default

The interface avoids clutter and makes common actions easy to repeat.

Privacy visible

Users can see and control backup, export, deletion, analytics, and app lock.

Clinician-aware

Doctor prep summaries and visit follow-ups help users bring clearer context.

Safety bounded

AI assists with explanation and routing, not diagnosis.

Remi is designed for ordinary health days and serious follow-up moments.

That means clear boundaries, warm UX, and practical records.
